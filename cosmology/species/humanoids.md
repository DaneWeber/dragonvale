# Humanoids

## Dwarves

Craft is life for a dwarf. Dwarves are industrious, loyal, and dedicated. While their greed is legendary, so is their dedication to excellence in their adopted pursuit.

### Old Dwarves

Old dwarves are driven to work and be useful until the very end, even as their bodies fail and their movement slows. A dwarf at the end of their natural lifespan will petrify in place, becoming a stone statue capturing their final moment of labor and dedication.

### Dwarves are Children of the Earth

There are no "female" dwarves as far as non-dwarves can tell. Dwarves are "female" if they are able to craft precious metal statues of a miniature dwarf. Dwarves reproduce when one carries a precious metal statue she has carved for at least 200 days. Another dwarf must find a precious gemstone (cut or uncut) and carry it for at least 1,000 days. Once the statue and stone have been carried long enough, they are imbued with their dwarf-parent's soul energy. The dwarf parents then ritually place the gem on the statue's chest and press. If successful, the gem merges into the statue and the entire thing becomes flesh and blood, breathing, alive, and growing.

### Dwarf Origins

Dwarves were created by elemental spirits through the machinations of dragons. The dragons were greedy for works of fine metals and precious stones and the native halflings had little desire to mine or craft them.

## Elves

Elves claim to be "the first people" and to have arrived from the stars.

Elves are, as a people, far-sighted, with a view to long-term results. While they cannot compete with Dragons and Devils in working for ages to accomplish a complex plan, they do think and act on another level beyond the short-sighted goals of the other populous humanoids. Nearly every grand pursuit has been accomplished by elves, including great cities, ocean-going fleets, and towering libraries, but always in the particular way of the elves, which seeks to integrate all worthy goals into an coherent whole, including aesthetics, lifecycles, contemplation, elegant functionality, elven flourishing, and the ultimate end of all things.

### Elven Lands

The elves have cities, islands, and other locales that cannot be found by ordinary people. Through innate senses, shared secrets, or magical means, elves are able to travel to these mysterious places. Arcanists claim that these are fey demi-planes connected to the prime through the ethereal, but these words do little to help anyone find their way there. Travel often requires a specific time, day, direction, or vehicle, but may also have additional keys or conditions required to pass.

### Elf Origins

Elves pre-date the historical cataclysms of Iothar and even the creation of the planet itself. They are survivors from a previous world that was completely lost and came to Iothar from the stars. The high elves believe they lost their world because the elves dominated the entire planet and so seek to make room for others and to protect their people in hidden places. The drow seek safety from future cataclysms deep below the surface, but do not believe world domination was the cause of their world's destruction.

## Gnolls

Gnolls are a race of bestial people known for their ferocity and pack mentality. Gnolls see divine significance in every action and believe they are a special people gifted by the gods with the will do what must be done, no matter the cost. Because of this special status, they do not usually set out to harm or kill their own kind. They believe that they, like all mortals, are flawed and doomed to eternal punishment. The screams and agony of their victims are offerings to the gods that they hope will be accepted in place of some of their own eternal suffering.

## Gnomes

Gnomes seek novelty in a way the other primary races do not. Gnomes have all of the curiosity, entrepreneurial spirit, and wanderlust that Halflings lack. Gnomes travel, adventure, and explore throughout the world with little devotion to their homelands. They set up businesses and enterprises that have never been tried before and attempt to accomplish feats of mechanics, alchemy, and magic that have never been seen before.

While they are inveterate tricksters who are not above tricking a man out of his money, their fey ancestry has faded as they have ceased being wild. In many ways, Gnomes are the domesticated fey. Slightly-less-domesticated Gnomes live in villages built into hills and trees that are hidden from view by fey illusions.

### Old Gnomes

As gnomes age, they become more eccentric and whimsical. Senile gnomes become lost in illusions and dreams, failing to distinguish reality from fantasy. Where an elderly human or halfling may hope to die peacefully in their sleep, gnomes lose any fear of death, disregard risk, and pursue wild experiments. A "typical" end may involve an explosion and plume of smoke from their workshop, leaving behind no evidence of their body but only the remnants of their final experiment.

### Gnomes are Children of the Earth

Gnomish children grow in the soil like plants for their first six months, forming a connection with the earth drawing upon the latent magical energies that permeate the land while fed sugar and sweets by their parents.

* They are planted by their mother and father after they have been baked and a spark of life imparted by their parents.
* Gnomes are first formed from a dough and their genius wrapped inside.
  * The genius of a gnome is something wonderful that the parents find or create, whether a piece of jewelry, a tea blend, an alchemical concoction, a poem, a bit of magic, or some other remarkable discovery.
    * The key is that the genius must be unique, never before given to a gnome. If the genius is not unique, the child will fail to grow and disintegrate into the soil.
    * The genius shapes the child and a meaningless one, such as writing "apoqwieyutosphasdgklh" on a piece of paper, while perhaps unique, will also result in the child's failure to grow and eventual disintegration into the soil.
  * The dough recipe is a family secret passed down through generations and must be formed into a shape that resembles its parents. Some of the ingredients come from the parents such as hair, breath, a kiss, or laughter.

## Goblins

### Goblin Ecology

Goblins carry "goblin eggs" within them that are terribly hardy and hatch a goblin larva soon after their parent/host dies. Goblins, hobgoblins, bugbears, and goblinqueens are the primary varieties of goblinkind, with the larger carrying more goblin eggs and the goblinqueen being able to excrete her eggs without dying. A goblin horde is an almost unkillable extinction-level threat to the world.

## Halflings

Halflings, as a people, find niches and stick to them. They enjoy the good life with small ambitions.

They are not a conquering race and they generally pack up and relocate rather than fight a war (although they have admirable courage and ingenuity when threatened). In general, Halfling enclaves try to either blend in or even disappear. A Halfling enclave would be pleased if neighboring communities did not know the Halflings were there, and were somewhat mystified by the Halflings that show up occasionally to trade. On the other hand, Halfling communities are usually quite hospitable to guests (although newcomers who stay cease being guests) and Halflings are considered as a whole to be very friendly and good-natured.

One curious thing about Halflings is that they seem to take to freshwater boating with greater pleasure than any other race. This leads to a good number of them traveling as extended families and calling their fleet of small boats home, rather than any stable location.

### Halfling Origins

Iothar is the halfling home world where this race of small folk were created and where they survived and thrived for ages, avoiding danger and finding safe havens. The other races are guests of the halflings, even though they do not acknowledge this or always reciprocate the hospitality.

### Human Origins

Humans are relatively new to Iothar. The Age of Man began only a few thousand years ago. They arrived from another world, bringing the common tongue of Sigil with them.

## Kobolds

An attempt by dragons to create a better version of the native halflings, combining draconic traits with halfling characteristics to produce servants who would carve lairs, fill armies, and craft beautiful works. Unfortunately for the dragons, while kobolds are ambitious, industrious, and comfortable underground, they are self-serving like a dragon and try to avoid conflict like a halfing. If anything, their sense of self-preservation might be their defining trait, attempting to use stealth, traps, and ranged weapons to stay out of danger, only fighting to the death when a worse death is the alternative.

## Orcs

Orcs innately understand that their first duty is to their own blood. The tribe is the family that shares that blood, and an orc will fight and die for kin in proportion to how much of itself it sees in them.

Beyond kin, loyalty is a wager. When a chief calls a raid, orcs weigh the odds and promised share to decide whether to join. Raiding parties and even hordes break apart when they can no longer promise sufficient benefits. The terms of shares are sacred and violating them destroys ones reputation, inciting revenge from the wronged and their kin without objection by other orcs.

The laws of other races are thought of as merely threats the strong make to the weak, to be ignored if you can get away with it. Orcs respect strength and cunning above all, and will follow those who demonstrate both, regardless of their lineage. Wariness and decisive action are the response to threats and opportunities alike. Few if any orcs are devoted to deities and those that are look for what they can get out of the arrangement.

The blood duty is to preserve, enrich, and propagate the blood lineage by eating, maturing, reproducing, and gaining martial prowess in order to better accomplish those ends. The literalness of their dedication to the success of the bloodline leads some scholars to hypothesize that orcs in fact are infested with a bloodborne parasite that compels them to help the parasite population spread and continue forever.

### Orc Origins

Orcs descend from the union of elves and one of first races of giants, both of whom wanted to preserve their bloodlines and were concerned about prophecies of their species' respective downfalls. The resulting orc race is rejected by both giantkind and elvenkind for being too much like the other. The ancient magic from both long-lived lineages afflicted orcs with short, intense lifespans utterly devoted to the preservation and propagation of their bloodline.
