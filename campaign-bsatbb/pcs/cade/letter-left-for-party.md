Dear Friends and Colleagues,

You must wonder what happened to me as clearly you are reading this letter now. When
we parted ways at the city gate, I went to me old friend Elwes Shope of Wizardry and Trinkets, a

also kept my spell book copies in his safe.

When I had paid him the platinum coin that Brother Hambone had given me to borrow, I
took the spell books and headed off toward the tavern. Fearing that the Black Lady may still
have interest in me, I made myself invisible and became visible once I entered the Bar. I sat
down and had a drink of some sour bitter ale that was, for lack of better words, if one could
distill the smell of Landorfs loin cloth. I still do not understand the taste of such things and why
the youth drink Ale like Swill. It is only the effects of what it does! I will never know. Perhaps that
is why they are a weaker, dumb and short-lived race.

Anyways, I decided to make conversation with the Bartender, and I mentioned that I was
in the company of Blackstaff and Landorf. The bartender did not take interest, but the man

door. I immediate ran after him, but as I got out outside, He looked at me, smiled, and vanished

make sure that they were fine.

However, As I was walking back to the inn, I realized I may have someone who can help
