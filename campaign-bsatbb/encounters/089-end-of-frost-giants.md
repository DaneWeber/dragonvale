Tryg casts his scrying spell and reports that he can see Black Stag, stripped of his armor and shirt, sweating profusely, and wearing his cloak on his antlers as a make-shift tent. He is sitting on black rock. Just in front of Black Stag is the Giantish word "Entry" carved into the ground.

Black Stag appears terribly uncomfortable, probably in danger of heatstroke, but not in immediate peril.

Tryg stops scrying and begins identifying the lever and its function. During this whole time, the party and their many retainers gather up all of the valuables owned by the frost giants and otherwise acquired in the rift. The list of such items will follow. Among the loot, one item has an obvious function: the greatsword belonging to the frost giant sorceress chills the air around it and could keep Black Stag safe, and possibly even permit him to travel through greater heat than he is currently experiencing.

Tryg identifies the lever as causing those within the alcove to be shifted to a specific location on the Inner Plane of Fire. Tryg explains that from what he saw of Black Stag, Black Stag is quite likely on the Plane of Fire, but within an "oasis" where there is a pocket of earth and air and the heat, while still great, is not of the intensity found in the majority of the plane.

While there could be a companion device at the destination, this lever causes a one-way trip.

Surfeit has the ability to travel between this Material Plane and the Inner Plane of Fire and back at will, but he arrives between 5 and 500 miles distant from his target location. He can thus make a delivery and then return, and just need to fly back to the rift.
