# Cantons of Hallstadt

The thirteen cantons of the Hallstadt Confederacy operate independently of each other. The articles of confederation provide for certain relations between the cantons and for a united defense of Hallstadt against outside invasion.

## Reichsfreyt

Capital: King's Keep (Metropolis of 25,001+ adults)
Size: 13th largest (400 sq. mi.), 1st most populous (40,000 vassals)
Lord Protector: Jovrim Reed-Dytain

King's Keep is the capitol city of Hallstadt where the cantons gather for united action. While it supplies a fair amount of its own food, it is reliant upon trade with the other cantons in order to feed itself. The keep after which the city is named is itself a tremendous structure which serves as the manor of the Lord Protector of Reichsfreyt.

## Helvetia

Capital: Watcher's Tower (Small City of 5,001-12,000 adults)
Size: 4th largest (1400 sq. mi.), 7th most populous (14,000 vassals)
Lord Protector: Jovrim Reed-Dytain

The western-most canton, Helvetia watches the gap in the mountain ring. A large river flows out of the valley and commerce is transacted up and down the river. To the east is an odd desert named Ashardalon's Plain which is a desert of fine gray sand. While hardly a treasure itself, valuable gems have been found beneath the flat sand. This plain is shared with the Kyburg canton. The entire canton of Helvetia is largely unforested, contained rolling hills. Watcher's Tower, the cantonal seat, sits in the middle of the pass next to the river and acts as the fortress guarding the valley.

## Waldstatt

Capital: Woodward (Large Town of 2,001-5,000 adults)
Size: 1st largest (2200 sq. mi.), 11th most populous (3,000 vassals)
Lord Protector: Son of Stag, Viscount: Maiden

Contains the great depths of the forest and is quite dangerous. Woodward watches over trade with the Full Moon Empire through the mountains. The smaller town of Drylake lies to the north where smaller rivers come out of the mountains.

## Unterwalden

Capital: Blasingdell (Large Town of 2,021 adults)
Size: 8th largest (1000 sq. mi.), 12th most populous (2,000 vassals)
Lord Protector: Aidar Van Eck, Viscount: Oswin Meinrad

A few farms. Hunting and herding. Famous for sausage made from pigs herded in forest and boars hunted in the woods. Curzack's Keep sits high in the valley, and this massive and ancient fortress stands guard as it has for centuries.

## Jura

Capital: King's Retreat (Small Town of 901-2,000 adults)
Size: 12th largest (600 sq. mi.), 13th most populous (1,000 vassals)
Lord Protector: Verdigriss

Because of the excellent springs found immediately outside the town of King's Retreat a sizable number of people visit each year, making the inns quite profitable. There are no other settlements in Jura, and King's Retreat is responsible for farming the land about two miles in every direction. The rest of the canton is forested and encircled by a smooth ring of mountains. The springs feed the crystal-clear Crescent lake that glitters below the Zircon, Treleni's fabled tower that is said to contain mind-bending rooms that challenge one's sanity.
