# Iospace

Crystal sphere containing the world of Iothar.

1. Iothar (center of system, earth body primary world)
2. Moon (earth body)
3. Sun (fire body)
4. River (water body)
   1. Water body ring that flows in an enormous circle around Iothar, beyond the Sun, aligned with Iothar's equator.
   2. The core of the ring rotates faster than the surface.
   3. The Sun comes close to the river at two points which boil into steam when it does. The portion far from the Sun cools to ice.
5. Visitors (celestial bodies with irregular orbits that make occasional approaches to Iothar)
   1. Erythnul, the Goblin Star (earth body)
      1. Home of the goblin race.
   2. Glittercloud (air body)
      1. Smoke, radiance, lightning, and air.
      2. Leaves trail across the sky.
6. Vana (dark body)
   1. Blots out the light of distant stars when it passes in front of them.
   2. Warps the light of distant stars when it passes near them.
7. Crown Jewels
   1. Ring of faint lights in an orbit at a right angle to the equator's plane, the furthest from Iothar.
   2. Each star is in fact a world encased in its own crystal sphere, everything within it completely isolated from wildspace.
   3. These worlds are held in stasis, unaffected by the passage of time or external forces.

## Constellations

The distant lights in the firmament area patterns in Io's scales that shift with its slow looping through its complex knot around Iospace. Some short-lived but bright gaps appear when Io's coils pull apart, allowing the phlogiston to shine through. The blast of phlogiston disrupts divine magic.

Iostars
  - Beings that cause one of Io's scales to glow.
  - Watch what happens on Iothar and in Iospace.
  - May fall to Iothar.
